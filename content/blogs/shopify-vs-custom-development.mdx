Choosing between Shopify and a custom e-commerce solution is one of the most important architectural decisions you'll make. Let's break it down.

## The Case for Shopify

### Pros
- **Speed to Market** - Launch in days, not months
- **Managed Infrastructure** - No server management, security updates, or hosting concerns
- **Built-in Features** - Payments, inventory, shipping integrations out of the box
- **Cost Predictability** - Monthly subscription model
- **App Ecosystem** - Thousands of apps for extended functionality

### Cons
- **Design Limitations** - Customization has boundaries
- **Transaction Fees** - Additional fees on top of payment processor fees
- **Lock-in** - Your data and customizations are tied to the platform
- **Scalability Costs** - Enterprise plans get expensive fast

## The Case for Custom Development

### Pros
- **Complete Control** - Every pixel, every feature, every decision is yours
- **Performance** - Build exactly what you need, nothing more
- **Integration** - Connect to any external system seamlessly
- **Unique Experience** - No template-like feel
- **Long-term Cost** - Often cheaper at scale with high-volume stores

### Cons
- **Higher Initial Investment** - More development time and cost upfront
- **Maintenance Burden** - You're responsible for everything
- **Longer Timeline** - Months instead of days or weeks

## When to Choose Shopify

Shopify makes sense when:

- You need to launch quickly
- Your team is small without dedicated developers
- Your requirements fit within Shopify's ecosystem
- Budget is constrained for initial development
- You don't have complex custom requirements

## When to Choose Custom

Custom development is the right choice when:

- You have unique business logic that doesn't fit Shopify
- Performance at scale is critical
- You need deep integrations with existing systems
- You have a development team or budget for ongoing maintenance
- Brand differentiation is a key competitive advantage

## Our Recommendation

For most small to medium businesses starting out, **Shopify is the right choice**. It lets you focus on your product and customers rather than infrastructure.

For established businesses with specific needs, unique customer experiences, or scale requirements, **custom development pays dividends**.
